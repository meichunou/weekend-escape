# Storylines

**A · "Friday 18:30" (picked).** A moment in someone's day + object-to-object chain. Friday 18:30, a free weekend and €150; the tabs pile up with no answer; one tap on "Find my weekend" swallows them; the real quiz, three taps; 14 destinations get stamped out until Brussels lands at ~€136; the plan arrives at 18:31. Sound: effects on every action, riser into one bass hit on the match.

**B · "Departure board" (the bold one).** Type carries the argument + one frame that rebuilds. The whole film is a split-flap departure board; the three answers filter the rows live until one line is left: BRUSSELS ~€136. Mechanical flap sounds, cuts on the beat.

**C · "€150".** A number lands. The number 150 is an object that splits into train, nights and food; each piece opens a scene. More graphic and abstract, less product UI.
